# Code of conduct

Lighthouse welcomes contributions regardless of background, identity or experience.
Treat participants respectfully, give specific technical feedback, and allow room
for mistakes and differing views. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse,
unwanted sexual attention and disclosure of another person's private information
are unacceptable.

This policy applies to the project's issues, pull requests, reviews and other
project-managed community spaces. Maintainers may remove abusive content, limit
participation, or block accounts to protect participants. They should apply these
measures consistently and keep sensitive report details private.

## Reporting and enforcement

A maintained private conduct-report address is pending. This document remains a
local draft until that contact is supplied; it does not claim a private maintainer
inbox is already available. Do not post personal or harassment-report details in
public issues or use the vulnerability form for unrelated conduct reports.

For content violating GitHub's own policies, GitHub also provides its independent
platform reporting process. That process does not replace the project's pending
maintainer contact. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).
